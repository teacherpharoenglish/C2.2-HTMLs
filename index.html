Part 2 · Random speaking challenge

Click Generate question. Each round randomly selects a discussion question, one phrasal verb, and one idiom from the course inventory. Use both expressions in your answer.

Instructions
  1. Read the question and the two required expressions.
  2. Answer in complete sentences and use both expressions naturally.
  3. Explain your reasoning and give a specific example where possible.
  4. There is no model answer for the random questions: produce your own response.
Worked example · How to do the task

Question: Your manager supports a decision you strongly disagree with. What would you do?

Phrasal verb to use
iron out
Idiom to use
rock the boat
Example response

“I would try to iron out our differences in a private conversation first. If the decision could seriously harm the team, I would be willing to rock the boat and explain my concerns respectfully.”

Notice: the response uses both required expressions naturally. The random challenges will not show sample answers.

Part 3 · Interactive cooperative game

THE NEXORA FILES

Four advanced missions. One corporate crisis with incomplete and potentially conflicting evidence. Distinguish fact from inference, test competing explanations, and defend a risk-aware recommendation to earn XP and unlock each file.

Players
2–7 students
Missions
4 unlockable files
Team score
0 XP

Your mission

Nexora Technologies has discovered a discrepancy between its client-facing deck and internal estimate. A vendor requirement may explain part of the change, but approval, awareness, and client reliance remain uncertain. Your team must assess the evidence without jumping to conclusions and recommend a defensible course of action.

Timeline: Friday — client presentation last edited. Monday — internal budget updated. Tuesday — vendor cost impact confirmed. The Friday event refers to the first week; Monday and Tuesday refer to the following week.

Team objective
Earn 400 XP to win
400 XP remaining

Correct answer: earn the mission XP. Use a hint: lose 25 XP. Each incorrect attempt: lose 10 XP. Reach 400 XP by the end to complete the mission.

Mission progress

Team debrief

Once all files are unlocked, give a 60-second recommendation to the CEO. Use at least one idiom and one phrasal verb from the vocabulary above.

Facilitator notes

The evidence establishes a $60,000 discrepancy and a sequence of events. The vendor added a security-testing requirement; its final cost impact was confirmed Tuesday. The evidence does not prove intentional concealment. A strong recommendation is to verify the final figures, correct the proposal, and communicate transparently with the client.
