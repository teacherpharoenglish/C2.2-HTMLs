01 · BEFORE YOU WATCH

What Lurks Behind Halloween?

Talk about the questions below. Share what you already know, make predictions and compare ideas. There are no wrong answers at this stage.

QUESTION 01

What images, feelings or traditions do you associate with Halloween?

QUESTION 02

Where do you think Halloween comes from? What makes you think so?

QUESTION 03

Do people in your culture have traditions connected with remembering the dead or warding off evil spirits?

Need a hand? You can give a short answer, describe an example, tell a story or explain your theory. Try starting with: “I associate it with…” · “I think it may come from…” · “In my culture, we…” · “One possible explanation is…”

02 · AFTER YOU WATCH

The Story Behind the Shadows

Work through these tasks together. Everyone can contribute: give a simple answer, add details, make connections or challenge an idea.

TASK A · REMEMBER & RECONSTRUCT

Put the events in historical order

Number the events from 1 (earliest) to 4 (latest), based on the video.

TASK B · MAKE CONNECTIONS

One celebration, many influences

Choose one question and discuss it with a partner or the group.

  1. Which historical tradition or detail did you find most surprising? Why?
  2. How has Halloween changed over time? What might explain those changes?
  3. Can you compare Halloween with a celebration or tradition from your own culture?
Ways to develop your answer: Give an example · Explain your reason · Compare two traditions · Consider another point of view · Question an assumption. You do not need to do all of these.
